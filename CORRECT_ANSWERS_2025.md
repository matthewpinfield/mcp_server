### 1. New Orleans Terror Attack

### 2. Tesla Cybertruck Explosion
**CORRECT ANSWER**: Tesla Cybertruck containing firework mortars and gas canisters exploded outside Trump International Hotel Las Vegas, injuring 7 people. Driver died from self-inflicted gunshot before explosion. FBI investigating as possible terrorist attack.

### 4. American Airlines Collision
**CORRECT ANSWER**: 67 feared dead after American Airlines jet collided with Army Black Hawk helicopter near Washington D.C. Both aircraft crashed into Potomac River.

### 6. Nintendo Switch 2
**CORRECT ANSWER**: Nintendo officially revealed Nintendo Switch 2 video game console, successor to Nintendo Switch.

### 7. Montenegro Shooting

### 8. Hughes Fire California
**CORRECT ANSWER**: Hughes Fire ignited and spread to over 5,000 acres in less than 4 hours, triggering evacuation orders around Castaic Lake in Los Angeles County.
